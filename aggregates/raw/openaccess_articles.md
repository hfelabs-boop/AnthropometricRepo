## Notes

* **pmc7767597-iran-military** (2014, The International Journal of Occupational and Environmental Medicine): Anthropometric Characteristics of Iranian Military Personnel and their Changes over Recent Years. Tables 1-2 with mean, SD, range, percentiles for 90 dimensions; 35 unambiguous dimensions + weight + BMI extracted; several printed percentile typos in the paper (e.g. elbow height sitting p95, functional leg length p75) were not used
* **pmc4691302-iran-military-dexa** (2015, Asian Journal of Sports Medicine): How Accurate Are the Anthropometry Equations in in Iranian Military Men in Predicting Body Composition?. Body-fat prediction equations vs DEXA; height, weight, BMI, DEXA %fat extracted; age omitted (text 35.5 vs table 35.8)
* **pmc4592759-iran-recruits-mtss** (2015, Asian Journal of Sports Medicine): Studying the Relation Between Medial Tibial Stress Syndrome and Anatomic and Anthropometric Characteristics of Military Male Personnel. MTSS case/control groups; age, height, weight per group
* **pmc4921355-india-armed-forces** (2009, Medical Journal Armed Forces India): . Tables 1-3 height/weight/BMI by age group (text obtained via WebFetch summary, consistent with abstract figures 171.01 cm, 70.16 kg, 20.52 kg/m2); n for 48-52 group not given; publisher copyright, PMC free full text (not CC)
* **ijasm2025-india-armed-forces-surveillance** (2025, Indian Journal of Aerospace Medicine): Anthropometric surveillance and obesity prevalence in Armed Force personnel: A cross-sectional study. Overall mean height, weight, umbilical girth, BMI only; subgroup SDs in paper are dispersion of group means so not extracted; PDF saved
* **pmc11020664-germany-bundeswehr** (2024, Journal of Occupational Medicine and Toxicology (London, England)): Prevalence of obesity and associated health risks in soldiers of the German Armed Forces. Age, BMI, waist circumference mean/SD/median for pooled sample; sex-specific stats not tabulated
* **pmc8909950-poland-armed-forces** (2022, International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health): Assessment of Risk Factors for Development of Overweight and Obesity among Soldiers of Polish Armed Forces Participating in the National Health Programme 2016–2020. Table 1 All column extracted; BMI-category subgroups not extracted
* **pmc10691415-poland-armed-forces-branches** (2023, International Journal of Occupational Medicine and Environmental Health): Evaluation of health behaviors of Polish Army soldiers in relation to demographic factors, body weight and type of armed forces. Table 1 by branch: age, weight, height, BMI
* **pmc10608823-poland-flying-personnel** (2023, Metabolites): Body Composition and Cardiovascular Risk: A Study of Polish Military Flying Personnel. Table 1 total column: anthropometry and BIA body composition; waist and hip circumference included
* **pmc12772597-finland-soldiers** (2025, BMJ Military Health): Physical activity, physical fitness and cardiometabolic health among Finnish military workers. Table 2 male soldiers extracted (civilians and women not extracted)
* **pmc13331485-czech-artillery** (2025, Military Medicine): Obesity and Health Risk Factors in Czech Artillery Soldiers. Table 1 men and women with DXA body composition
* **pmc6007356-swiss-conscripts** (2018, Evolution, Medicine, and Public Health): Increasing variability of body mass and health correlates in Swiss conscripts, a possible role of relaxed natural selection?. Table 1 height, weight, BMI by conscription era 1875-79, 1932-39, 1994, 2010-12 with P5/median/P95
* **pmc6192153-brazil-army-cadets** (2018, BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders): Can anthropometric, body composition, and bone variables be considered risk factors for musculoskeletal injuries in Brazilian military students?. Table 1 total sample; injured/non-injured splits not extracted
* **pmc8171865-chile-naval-cadets** (2021, PLoS ONE): Effects of a period without mandatory physical training on maximum oxygen uptake and anthropometric parameters in naval cadets. Table 1 by sex; weight pre-test from Table 2; PDF saved (PLOS, CC BY)
* **pmc11270323-angola-military-cadets** (2024, Journal of Functional Morphology and Kinesiology): Effects of 34 Weeks of Military Service on Body Composition and Physical Fitness in Military Cadets of Angola. Pre-training values; sexes pooled
* **pmc7663089-taiwan-af-academy-student-pilots** (2020, International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health): Roles of Physiological Responses and Anthropometric Factors on the Gravitational Force Tolerance for Occupational Hypergravity Exposure. Table 1 pass/fail groups (age, height, weight, BMI)
* **pmc8620872-saudi-armed-forces-applicants** (2021, Medicines): Recent Trends of Metabolic Syndrome and Its Components in Military Recruits from Saudi Arabia. Only age and height extracted; printed weight/BMI/waist SDs and ranges are internally inconsistent (waist mean 65.7 with CI 76-79)
* **pmc13374411-swiss-conscripts-2006-2024** (2026, BMJ Open): Trends in physical fitness performance and body mass index of Swiss military conscripts, 2006–2024: a cross-sectional study. Only adjusted model means with CIs (BMI 23.2 in 2006), no raw mean/SD tables; nothing extracted
* **pmc13346719-norway-conscripts** (2026, Journal of Public Health Research): Health literacy, dietary behavior and body mass index in male and female Norwegian conscripts. A cross-sectional study. Table gives mean and range only (height 183 M / 168 F cm, weight 81/66 kg), no SD; nothing extracted
* **pmc12535166-lithuania-volunteer-forces** (2025, BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders): Prevalence and risk factors of low back pain among Lithuanian national defense volunteer forces. Demographic tables categorical only; no anthropometric mean/SD
* **pmc9132859-korea-enlisted-bmi** (2022, BMJ Military Health): Effects of military life on changes in body mass index of enlisted men: a cross-sectional study. Tables show BMI change by subgroup only; no absolute anthropometric means; authors Lee, Bae, Oh et al.
* **pmc8170814-spain-aeronautical** (2021, Military Medical Research): Hydration status, body composition, and anxiety status in aeronautical military personnel from Spain: a cross-sectional study. Anthropometry only inside hydration-status comparisons (not extracted); low priority
* **pmc10572769-finland-navy** (2023, Healthcare): Effects of 12-Month Training Intervention on Physical Fitness, Body Composition, and Health Markers in Finnish Navy Soldiers. Table 1 demographics give mean(SD) by control/intervention/team but sex composition not stated so not extracted
* **pmc12701076-korea-af-cadets** (2025, NPJ Microgravity): Morphological perspective of ergonomic implications of hand function and cartilage thickness in Air Force cadets. Table 1 injury-history groups only (small n not verified); not extracted
* **pmc7922340-australia-heat-tolerance** (2021, Medicina): Individual Anthropometric, Aerobic Capacity and Demographic Characteristics as Predictors of Heat Intolerance in Military Populations. Mixed military/civilian, heat-tolerance groups; not extracted
* **pmc10625793-saudi-medical-officers** (2023, Cureus): The Impact of a 10-Week Military Training Course on Saudi Medical Recruits' Fitness and Physical Activity Levels. Only intro/methods text read; tables were protocol tables; not extracted

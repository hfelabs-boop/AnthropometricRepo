# The posture editor: engine and Posture panel

The Posture tab of the Digital human page picks a posture from the library, shows and changes the current one and edits it joint by joint or by dragging handles in the 3D view. Two modules do that:

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `site/js/human/editor.js` | The engine: state, placement, history, custom postures, IK drag controller, handle layer. No DOM (the handle layer needs a viewport, which tests fake). Published as `ctx.posture`. |
| `site/js/human/panels/posture.js` | The panel (`id: "posture"`). Everything above `// ---- panel` is pure and tested in Node. |
| `site/human-posture.css` | Styles, prefix `hm-pe-`; injected once by the panel with a `<link rel="stylesheet">`. Colours are the page tokens (`--accent`, `--bad`, ...), never literals. |
| `tests/human3d/editor.test.mjs` | Node tests of the engine and of the panel's pure helpers (real rig, fake viewport, manual clock). |
| `tests/human3d/test_posture_panel.py` | Playwright tests of the panel on the real site. |
| `tests/human3d/pages/editor-sheet.html` | Every library posture as a stick-figure card on one page (visual check of the cards). |

Contract with the rest: DESIGN.md 3.3 and 3.3b (panels and the posture library), POSE.md (pose model and placement), IK.md (solver), POSTURES.md (library). Where this document and the code differ, the code wins; the differences found are listed at the end.

## Using the panel

**Library.** A search box (every word must occur in the name, description, tags or category, accents and case ignored: the same rule as `postures.js` `search()`), 13 category chips with counts (the counts follow the search and the chosen tags; a chip with 0 is greyed), a Custom chip once there is a custom posture (when the browser's store holds entries that could not be read, a notice above the list says how many and that a copy of the store is kept, see Custom postures), and tag chips (closed by default, most used first). The list is windowed: about 15 rows exist in the DOM however long it is. Each card has a canvas stick figure of the posture on the current body (side or front view, whichever shows more; a seat is drawn as a line; drawn lazily, cached per body and posture, redrawn on a profile or theme change), a support badge (stands, sits, kneels, lies...), a validator badge (`ok`, `ok, notes`, `fails`, from `validatePosture` on the current body, computed lazily) and a source badge (ISO 7250-1, illustrative...). Click or Enter selects. Keys on the list: Up and Down, Home, End, Page Up and Down move, Enter applies; `aria-activedescendant` names the active card, a polite live region says "Showing ... Sits, feet on the floor.". Escape in the search box clears it.

**Current posture.** Name with an "edited" dot, Undo and Redo (also Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y while the tab is shown and no field has the focus), Reset (back to the posture the pose started from), Neutral, Mirror, Copy L to R and R to L, Save, Delete, Export and Import. Save as custom under the posture's own name (when it is a saved one) updates it in place; under any other name it makes a new posture, and when another saved posture already has that name the form asks once ("Press Save again to keep both") and nothing is ever overwritten by a name. Delete (only for a saved custom posture, with a confirmation) leaves the figure as it is and puts the focus on Save. Placement: what carries the weight (the support of the posture, changeable), the surface (floor, seat or free), the seat height as a number in the chosen unit, 0 to 1500 mm (or "Seat of the posture"), a "Sit on" list of the seats of the Scene tab (the figure then follows the chair when it is moved, turned or raised), "Stay on the floor or seat" and the heading. Live readouts: balance (margin of the centre of mass to the support polygon, with the 40 mm rule of POSE.md), the gap of each carrier to its surface, joints outside their range, the last clamp, the key angles (trunk inclination, hip, knee, elbow, upper arm, eye height; from `metrics.js`) and the ergonomic flags.

**Edit.** Switches: Enforce range of motion, Symmetric (an edit also moves the other side; the eyes keep looking the same way) and IK handles. Eight region tabs (one tab panel each, only the selected one shown, so every tab's `aria-controls` names an element that exists): Spine and pelvis, Head and eyes, Left and Right arm, Left and Right hand, Left and Right leg. Every degree of freedom is a slider and a number field: the track shades the range of motion (with the range not enforced the margin beyond it is a plain grey; the thumb, the field and the label turn red once the value is outside the range), the zero is a button, the limits are written under it. Arrow keys step 1 degree, Shift or Page Up and Down 10, Alt 0.1; a pointer drag or a run of key presses is one history step. A number typed beyond the range is limited and the row says so ("Limited to 150° by the range of motion"); an empty field changes nothing. The spine tab has the pelvis tilt (the orientation of the whole body, as in POSE.md), a total per region (lumbar, thoracic, cervical: flexion, lateral bend, rotation; a change of the total is shared by the vertebrae with the weights of skeleton.js on top of what each already has, so the edit of a single vertebra survives it) and, in a closed details element, the 24 vertebrae. The hand tabs have a grip preset (relaxed, fist, pinch, ...; "no preset" once a finger is edited), a curl slider per finger and the finger joints. The Skeleton tab's inspector shows the same editable sliders for the selected joint (the panel replaces its read-only renderer with `setJointDetailsRenderer`, see below).

**Handles in the 3D view** (switch "IK handles"): blue spheres at the palms, green at the feet, an amber ring at the pelvis, a violet sphere on the chest (trunk lean), grey diamonds at the elbows and knees (swivel), and a pink cross for the look-at target once that is switched on. A handle keeps one size on the screen. Hovering names it; dragging moves it on the plane through it that faces the camera (the pelvis in "Move figure" mode: on the horizontal plane through it when the camera looks down on it by 17 degrees or more, otherwise, in the front, back, left and right views, whose horizontal plane is edge-on, on the plane facing the camera; the reach of a drag is limited to 4 m); the orbit controls are held during the drag; Escape, a second pointer, a window that loses the focus or a hidden page cancels it and puts everything back, and a pointer move with no button down (a release that never arrived) ends it where it is; so does a drag the editor cancels (a new profile, an undo): the layer lets go of the orbit controls at once. Releasing is one history step ("Move right hand"). When the target cannot be reached the handle turns red and its label says how far short it falls ("Right hand: 36 mm short"); the figure then takes the closest pose inside the ranges. Pins: "Keep the feet in place" (on) holds both feet while the pelvis moves and while a knee is swivelled; "Keep the hands in place" (off) holds both hands while the pelvis or the chest moves. Keyboard: in "Handle options and keyboard controls" every handle is a button; focusing selects it (and the inspector shows it), the arrow keys nudge 10 mm in the view's right and up directions, Shift 100 mm, Alt 1 mm, Page Up and Down move away from and toward the camera. Look-at: a switch, "Look at the selected object" (uses the Scene tab's selection) and "Look at the measurement point" (the Measure tab's pick).

The panel keeps small preferences (last tab, IK handles, options) in `localStorage` key `hm-posture-prefs-v1`; every access is guarded and the panel works without it.

## Engine API (`ctx.posture`)

```js
import { editorFor, peekEditor } from "./editor.js";      // the shell has no hook for it: any panel can reach the editor of its viewport
const ed = editorFor(ctx);                                  // created on first use; also ctx.posture and (non-enumerable) ctx.state.postureEditor
ed.apply("sit-upright-90");                                 // library or custom id, or a Posture object
const drag = ed.createDrag("palm_R"); drag.begin(p0); drag.move(p1); drag.end();
```

`createEditor({ getRig, getBody, ik, metrics, viewport, ctx, storage, scheduler, reducedMotion, library, coalesceMs })` builds an editor without the registry (tests do). Units: metres and degrees, +Y up, +Z in front of the figure, +X its left.

**Posture.** `apply(idOrPosture, { animate, keepPlacement, record, label })` returns `{ ok, id, name, kind, clamped, done }`: expandPosture, clampPose, then placement on its support, animated over 350 ms unless `animate: false`, the Animate option is off or the user prefers reduced motion (`done` settles when it ends; any edit, undo or a new apply finishes a running animation first, so the state always matches the history). Throws for an unknown id. `neutral()`, `resetToLibrary()`, `mirror()`, `copySide("L" | "R")`, `getPosture(id)` (a copy), `base` (`{ id, name, kind: "library" | "custom" | "imported" | "neutral" }`), `modified` (a comparison of the pose with the clamped base, not a flag). The shell's status bar is told through `ctx.setPosture({ id, name })`: the posture's own id and name while the pose equals it, `{ id: null, name: "<name> (edited)" }` once it does not.

**Editing.** `setJoint(jointId, dof, value, { symmetric, clamp, record })` (dof: index or key; returns `{ applied, clamped }`), `resetJoint`, `setRegion("lumbar" | "thoracic" | "cervical", [flex, lat, rot])` (the new totals; the difference to the present totals is shared out over the vertebrae on top of what they have), `setRoot({ heading, flex, lat, rot, ... })` (a position only when the placement is free; with nothing to set it does nothing and records nothing), `setHeading`, `setAnchor({ x, z })`, `setPose({ root, angles }, { clamp })` (the semantics of `rig.setPose`: joints not listed become 0), `setGrip(side, name)`, `gripOf(side)`, `setFingerAngle`, `setFingerCurl`, `fingerCurl`. All of them re-ground the figure on its support after the change, record one history step (`record: false` while a slider is dragged, then `commit(label, key)` or use `beginGesture`), refresh `clamps` and emit `change`.

**Placement.** `place` (`{ support, mode: "floor" | "seat" | "free", stick, heading, anchor, seat: { kind: "auto" | "height" | "object", hint, height, objectId, supportId, name }, carriers }`), `setSupport(name)`, `setMode(mode)`, `setSeatHeight(m)`, `useSeatOfPosture()`, `sitOn(objectId, supportId, { face })`, `seatSurfaces()`, `seatHeight()`, `setStick(bool)`, `ground()`; `setOption(name, value)` and `options` for `enforceRom`, `animate`, `symmetric`, `pelvisMode` ("bend" | "move"), `trunkAssist`, `handFrame`, `footPoint` ("ball" | "heel"), `pinFeet`, `pinHands`, `stick`.

**History.** `undo()`, `redo()`, `canUndo`, `canRedo`, `history` (`{ canUndo, canRedo, undoLabel, redoLabel, steps, index }`), `beginGesture(label, key)` (returns `{ end() }`), `endGesture()`, `commit(label, key)`. 200 steps; a slider drag, a handle drag and a run of key presses on the same control within 600 ms are one step. A step holds pose, placement (support, mode, seat, anchor, heading, stick), the base posture and the look-at target, so undo puts the figure back where it stood, not only the angles. A step of another body (a new profile) is restored on the new body and re-grounded. A new profile in the middle of the 350 ms glide finishes the glide on the new figure (it ends in the posture, not half way, and is not "edited"), and an open slider gesture becomes a step.

**Custom postures and files.** `saveCustom(name, { category, description, overwrite })` (async; returns `{ ok, id, posture, persistent, warning?, error? }`, never throws for storage problems), `listCustom()`, `deleteCustom(id)`, `customProblems()`, `customPersistent`, `importPosture(fileOrTextOrObject, { save, animate, keepPlacement })` (returns `{ ok, name, warnings, errors, clamped }`), `exportPosture()` (the object of the Export tab's posture file), `exportPostureText()`.

**Readouts.** `info()` returns `{ base, modified, place, history, romViolations, clamps, balance, gaps, metrics, angles, flags, grips }`; `clamps` is the last list of `{ joint, dof, requested, applied }`.

**IK.** `handles()`, `handlePoint(id)`, `handleAvailable(id)`, `createDrag(id, { immediate })`, `cancelDrag()`, `dragging`, `nudgeHandle(id, [dx, dy, dz])`, `lookAtPoint(p)` (returns null and changes nothing for anything that is not a point), `setLookTarget(p)`, `lookTarget()`, `toggleLook(on)`, `lookActive`, `defaultLookTarget()`, `ikReady`, `loadIk()`, `warmUp()`, `rigPoint(landmarkId)`. `createHandleLayer({ viewport, editor, colors, onSelect })` builds the 3D handles (the panel does; `editor.handleLayer` is the panel's layer).

**Events** (`const off = ed.on(name, fn)`): `change { reason }`, `external` (the rig changed from elsewhere), `history`, `place`, `options`, `animation { state: "start" | "done" | "cancelled" }`, `custom`, `clamp { clamped }`, `drag { phase: "begin" | "move" | "end" | "cancel", handle, status }`, `look`, `rig` (the rig was replaced: a new profile). Listeners that throw are logged and do not stop the others. `dispose()` ends a drag and an animation and removes every listener it added (the panel refcounts: it disposes the editor when its last user leaves).

## The placement model

The rig holds the pose; the editor holds where it stands. After every edit the figure is put on its support again with `placePoseDetailed` (POSE.md): the carriers of the support (`SUPPORT_CARRIERS`: feet, seat and feet, knees, hands and knees, body, ...) touch the surfaces (floor, and a seat at the chosen height), and the horizontal **anchor** (the centroid of the carrier points) stays where it was, so bending the trunk or the hips neither lifts the figure off its seat nor lets it sink, and it does not slide. The anchor is read off the figure again after a foot or pelvis drag. `heading` turns the figure about the anchor. Mode "free" switches all of this off (nothing re-grounds; the pelvis handle then moves the figure in 3D); "Stay on the floor or seat" off keeps the pose changes but not the grounding. A seat taken from a sandbox object is followed while the figure sits on it (mode "seat" and a support with a seat): on the shell's `scene` event the anchor moves and turns with the chair and the figure is grounded on its new height; when the chair is deleted the seat height is kept and a toast says so. A figure that stands, kneels or sits on the floor does not follow the chair (the chair's bookkeeping is kept up to date so that nothing jumps when it sits again); applying a library posture that does not sit on a seat lets go of the chair altogether (its height stays as the seat height). **Which seat a posture gets:** the seat the user chose (a height, a chair) is kept by the postures whose seat adapts to the body (`hints.seat.height` "popliteal" or none: the ISO 7250-1 family), a posture that asks for a height of its own (a number: the mat of `sit-floor-long`, the high stool) gets that one, and `keepPlacement: true` (Reset) keeps whatever there is.

## IK drags

`createDrag(id)` returns `{ begin(point), move(point), end(), cancel(), status, active, handle }`. `begin` records the start pose and the grab offset, `move` stores the target and solves at most once per animation frame (`immediate: true` solves inside `move`: tests, the keyboard), `end` lets the figure arrive, polishes the result and records ONE history step, `cancel` restores the start pose and placement. The solver is `ik.js` with `DRAG_OPTIONS` (follow mode: no restarts, a few degrees per call, so a far target is approached over some frames and nothing jumps); `reference` is the pose at the drag start, so the figure stays as relaxed as it was.

`status`: `{ handle, label, reachable, state: "ok" | "moving" | "short", limited, amount, unit, short, message, errors, tolerance, ms, target }`. `moving` is "still on its way", `short` is a real shortfall (the red state: `short` is "36 mm short" or "12 mm off", `message` a sentence). Per handle:

* hand, foot (ball or heel): a position goal on `palm_centre_<s>`, `ball_centre_<s>` or `heel_sole_<s>`; the foot lies flat when it is within 3 cm of the floor; the free variables are the arm (and clavicle, and the trunk with "trunk assists") or the leg;
* pelvis, "bend": the root moves to the target and the legs, with the three sole points of both feet pinned where they were at the start (`ik.pinGoals`, fixed for the whole drag: re-reading them every frame drifts), follow; "move": the anchor moves, no solver;
* chest: a position goal on `chest_front` with the trunk free, hands pinned with "Keep the hands in place";
* elbow and knee (poles): the joint is moved on the circle the straight line shoulder-wrist (hip-ankle) allows, the hand or foot pinned; a straight limb has no swivel and the handle does nothing;
* look-at: `lookAt` goal (head, neck, eyes; the trunk with "trunk assists").

Two things the follow solver cannot do alone and the editor adds: when it is stuck in a corner of the ranges although the goal is within reach (a straight arm dragged toward the shoulder: the elbow would have to bend first) it runs a full solve with restarts from the drag-start pose (at most every 120 ms and every 3 cm of the target) and glides to that pose 8 degrees a frame; and a pelvis dragged down from straight legs gets a small knee bend seeded from the law of cosines (a straight knee is a singularity: without the seed the solver pushed the knee into its backward stop and the legs stayed straight). The drag ends with one ordinary local solve from where it stopped, and then a **relaxation**: the follow solver keeps whatever the path left in the redundant joints (a hand taken out and back ends with the forearm twisted 50 degrees), so the end solves the same point once more from the drag-start pose (`reference` = the start) and takes that pose when it is nearer the start by `RELAX_GAIN`; out and back leaves the arm, and down and home the legs, as they were. While the button is down and the pointer rests, a figure that is still on its way (the solver moves a few degrees a call) goes on frame by frame without a new pointer event, for at most 600 frames and only while it still moves. The pelvis "bend" drag is solved without the follow limit on every event (`solvePelvis`): the root is at the pointer and the pinned feet stay where they were at every step; where the legs cannot (a limit of motion) the root stays at the last place they could (found by bisection) and the handle says how far short the pelvis is.

## Custom postures

Stored in `localStorage` under `hm-custom-postures-v1` as `{ "version": 1, "postures": [ ... ] }`, at most 100 postures and 200 kB of text. A posture has the library's fields (`id` `custom-<slug>`, unique, `name` up to 60 characters, `category`, `tags`, `description`, `source`, `support`, `root`, `spine`, `joints`, `grips`, `contacts`, `hints`: see POSTURES.md); a saved one holds every non-zero joint by its own id (the eyes in the posture convention), the support and the seat height of the placement and the hints of the posture it began from. Everything read from storage or from a file goes through `sanitizePosture`: only known fields are copied (no key of the input is ever assigned except joint ids looked up as own properties of the joint table, so `__proto__` and `constructor` do nothing), angles must be finite numbers within 3600 degrees and the right length, joint ids must exist, ids must be kebab-case and, for the store, start with `custom-`, and the result must still pass `expandPosture`. Corrupt JSON, the wrong shape, an oversized store or hostile entries give a shorter list and a line in `customProblems()`, never an exception. What the first write after a load with unreadable entries is about to drop is kept: the text of the store as it was is copied to `hm-custom-postures-v1-unreadable` (`BACKUP_KEY`; the first copy is kept) before it is replaced, and the library shows a notice (`customProblemsText`) as long as the problems are there. The panel's preferences (`hm-posture-prefs-v1`) go through `cleanPrefs`: known keys of the right type only. When the browser will not store (private window, quota) the postures last for the session in memory and the result says `persistent: false` with a plain-language warning.

A posture file (Export and Import) is the one of `export.js` (`app: "digital-human-posture"`, the pose, the body summary, the posture); `importPosture` reads it with `parsePostureFile`, shows the pose on the figure and, with `save: true`, keeps it as a custom posture. `exportPosture()` adds what `export.js` does not know: the support of the placement and the seat height.

## Decisions, deviations and limits

* **Pelvis tilt** is the orientation of the whole body (`root.flex`, `lat`, `rot` of POSE.md), not a pelvis-over-trunk tilt: the figure stays on its support while it turns. A tilt of the pelvis alone is the lumbar slider.
* **Straight limbs** have no swivel: the elbow and knee handles do nothing until the limb is bent.
* **A deep squat** with the feet flat is limited by the ankles (dorsiflexion 35 degrees in the model) and the trunk stays upright in "bend" mode; past that the legs twist to keep the feet in place and the handle turns red. The pelvis handle does not tilt the pelvis.
* **Seat recognition** for "Sit on" is a heuristic over the support surfaces of the sandbox (a surface 0.15 to 0.9 m high whose id or name says seat, bench, stool, lid, top, platform, deck or pad, and not foot, mat or shelf).
* **Live drag lag**: the follow solver moves a joint by at most 0.04 rad per call, so on a slow machine (software WebGL: a frame takes 100 ms) the figure lags the pointer while it moves and the label may say "short" before the figure has arrived; once the pointer rests the figure goes on by itself and the drag end settles the rest. On a real GPU it keeps up.
* **The posture list** is the one of `postures.js` at load time; categories with no postures yet are shown greyed with 0.
* `export.js` `posturePostureOf` guesses the support (feet or body) from the pose, so `exportPosture()` patches `posture.support` and `hints.seat.height` into the file; an `export.js` that took the placement would make that unnecessary.
* The Skeleton panel's footer still says "Read-only here: the Posture tab moves the joints"; while the Posture panel is mounted it replaces the joint inspector with editable sliders (`setJointDetailsRenderer` of panels/skeleton.js), so the sentence is only right when it is not.
* The shell has no `ctx.posture` hook of its own; the editor is reached with `editorFor(ctx)`, `peekEditor(ctx)` or `ctx.state.postureEditor` once the Posture tab has been opened.
